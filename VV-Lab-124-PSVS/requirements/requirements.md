# Polar Seed Vault System — Requirements

**Document ID:** PSVS-REQ  
**Version:** 1.0  

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| S1 | The system shall accept seed boxes only from registered depositors. |
| S2 | Each chamber shall support storage of multiple seed boxes, subject to its capacity limit. |
| S3 | Chambers 1–3 shall hold at most 50,000 boxes each. Chamber 4 shall hold at most 20,000 boxes. |
| S4 | Each box shall belong to exactly one depositor, and only that depositor may withdraw it under normal operating conditions. |
| S5 | During an emergency, the vault authority may authorize release of any box to a requesting party. |
| S6 | The system shall maintain safe storage temperatures for seed samples. |
| S7 | During normal operation, chamber temperature shall never exceed -18°C. |
| S8 | During authorized maintenance, a chamber may be warmed to 0°C. |
| S9 | Seed samples with low viability shall be flagged promptly for regeneration. |
| S10 | A depositor may withdraw at most 20% of its boxes of a given species per year. |
| S11 | A withdrawn box shall be redeposited only after re-inspection. |
| S12 | The system shall maintain an audit trail of all actions. |
