# Defect Log — Polar Seed Vault System

**Project:** VV-Lab-124-PSVS  
**Source requirements:** S1–S12  
**Review status:** Initial independent analysis; peer review pending

**Severity guide:** Critical = threatens authorization, safety, or a core policy; High = prevents reliable implementation/verification of a major rule; Medium = important operational or data-definition gap; Low = useful audit/operational detail with limited direct safety impact. Severity is a proposed review judgment and should be confirmed with the instructor or team.

| Defect ID | Req ID | Category | Description | Evidence | Severity | Suggested fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | S1 | Incompleteness | Registration eligibility and registration lifecycle are not defined. | “registered depositors” does not say what evidence establishes registration, whether it can expire, or how suspension is handled. | High | Define registration approval, unique depositor ID, active status, expiry, suspension, and validation at deposit time. | Open |
| D-002 | S2, S3 | Ambiguity | S2 describes a large capacity while S3 provides exact per-chamber limits, but the relationship between general capacity and individual chamber limits is not explicit. | S2 says “a large number”; S3 gives 50,000 for Chambers 1–3 and 20,000 for Chamber 4. | Medium | Treat S3 as the authoritative numeric capacity and replace “large number” in S2 with a reference to configured chamber limits. | Open |
| D-003 | S3 | Incompleteness | No rule defines whether a box can be moved between chambers or how capacity is checked during transfers. | S3 limits boxes per chamber but does not describe transfer handling or atomic capacity checks. | Medium | Specify transfer validation: destination capacity must be available before a move is committed; a box must not be counted in two chambers. | Open |
| D-004 | S4, S5 | Contradiction / missing exception precedence | Normal owner-only withdrawal and emergency release authority overlap without an explicit authorization precedence and process. | S4 says only the depositor may withdraw; S5 permits the vault authority to release any box to any requesting party. | Critical | Define emergency release as a narrowly scoped exception, including authorized decision-maker, emergency declaration, identity checks, reason, recipient, and post-event review. | Open |
| D-005 | S4 | Incompleteness | The requirement does not define whether ownership can be transferred or how ownership changes are recorded. | “belongs to exactly one depositor” does not define whether ownership transfer is prohibited or supported. | Medium | Specify whether ownership transfer is allowed; if allowed, require authorization, effective time, and audit records while preserving one-owner-at-a-time integrity. | Open |
| D-006 | S5 | Ambiguity | “In an emergency” and “any requesting party” are undefined. | S5 uses both phrases without eligibility rules, emergency types, or limits. | High | Define emergency types, who may declare one, eligible recipients, approval evidence, and mandatory safeguards. | Open |
| D-007 | S6, S7 | Vagueness / potential redundancy | S6 requires a “safe temperature” but gives no measurable range; S7 supplies only an upper limit. | S6 says “safe temperature”; S7 says “never rise above -18°C,” which does not define a lower bound or species-specific safe range. | High | Define an acceptable temperature interval and clarify whether S7 is the maximum-temperature limit within that interval. | Open |
| D-008 | S7, S8 | Contradiction | The unconditional wording of S7 conflicts with the maintenance exception in S8 unless operating states are distinguished. | S7 says “shall never rise above -18°C”; S8 permits warming to 0°C during maintenance. | Critical | Model normal and authorized-maintenance states explicitly; define when S8 overrides S7, allowed duration, monitoring, authorization, and return-to-normal criteria. | Open |
| D-009 | S7 | Non-verifiability / incompleteness | Temperature sampling frequency, sensor accuracy, and failure behavior are unspecified. | “shall never rise” is an absolute claim, but no measurement method, sampling interval, tolerance, or sensor-failure response is defined. | High | Specify calibrated sensor accuracy, sampling and alert intervals, tolerated measurement uncertainty, redundancy, and fail-safe response. | Open |
| D-010 | S8 | Incompleteness | Maintenance warming lacks authorization, duration, and restoration conditions. | S8 permits warming to 0°C but says nothing about who approves it, how long it can last, or when the chamber must be cooled again. | High | Define maintenance authorization, maximum duration, monitoring, affected-box handling, and a verified return-to-storage condition. | Open |
| D-011 | S9 | Vagueness / non-verifiability | “low viability” and “promptly” have no objective threshold or deadline. | S9 uses both terms without a viability percentage or maximum alert delay. | High | Set a validated viability threshold, measurement method, maximum alert latency, alert recipient, and regeneration workflow. | Open |
| D-012 | S9 | Incompleteness | The requirement does not define who confirms a viability result or what happens after a flag is raised. | S9 only requires a flag; it does not define review, acknowledgement, escalation, or closure. | Medium | Specify responsible role, acknowledgement deadline, escalation, regeneration decision, and closure evidence. | Open |
| D-013 | S10 | Ambiguity | The denominator for “20% of its boxes of a species” is undefined. | S10 does not say whether the percentage is calculated from opening-year holdings, current holdings, or total holdings at another fixed date. | Critical | Define the reference inventory snapshot and whether approved but not yet completed withdrawals count toward the limit. | Open |
| D-014 | S10 | Incompleteness | The annual period and reset rules are unspecified. | “per year” does not state calendar year, rolling 365 days, depositor fiscal year, or time zone. | High | Choose a precise annual accounting period and define its time zone and reset boundary. | Open |
| D-015 | S10 | Non-verifiability | The rule does not define rounding when 20% produces a fractional box count. | For example, 20% of 7 boxes is 1.4 boxes, but boxes are indivisible. | High | Specify rounding (for example, floor to the nearest whole box) and test boundary cases, including small inventories. | Open |
| D-016 | S11 | Incompleteness | “re-inspection” has no criteria, responsible role, or recorded outcome. | S11 requires re-inspection but does not define what is inspected or what constitutes a pass. | High | Define inspection criteria, inspector authority, pass/fail outcomes, rejection handling, and required records before redeposit. | Open |
| D-017 | S11, S12 | Incompleteness / traceability risk | The requirement does not explicitly require a link between withdrawal, re-inspection, and redeposit records. | S11 specifies re-inspection; S12 requires an audit trail but does not define event linkage. | Medium | Use a unique box ID and linked event IDs so the withdrawal, inspection result, and redeposit can be traced end-to-end. | Open |
| D-018 | S12 | Vagueness / non-verifiability | “good audit trail” has no defined scope or acceptance criteria. | S12 does not list events, mandatory fields, retention period, access controls, or tamper protection. | High | Define audit event coverage, actor, timestamp, box/chamber, action, outcome, reason, retention, access, and integrity controls. | Open |
| D-019 | S1, S12 | Incompleteness / traceability risk | The requirements do not explicitly require rejected deposit attempts to be audited. | S1 restricts deposits to registered depositors, but S12 does not state whether denied actions are recorded. | Medium | Require audit records for successful and rejected deposits, including actor, time, reason, and outcome. | Open |
| D-020 | S3, S12 | Incompleteness / traceability risk | Capacity-limit violations and rejected placement attempts are not explicitly tied to audit records. | S3 sets capacity limits; S12 does not define audit coverage for blocked operations or capacity alarms. | Low | Record capacity-related rejection and alert events, including chamber, observed count, limit, actor, and outcome. | Open |
